# Tvim 1.9.14 — Random Tvin Greetings

- 10 morning and 10 evening greetings in Russian and English.
- Each notification chooses a random greeting, avoiding consecutive repeats when multiple choices are available.
- Edit, add or remove choices in Settings → Tvin mode: one message per line, up to 30 choices per schedule.
- Existing custom greetings and scheduled times are preserved.

Install over the existing app to preserve data.
