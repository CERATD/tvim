# Tvim 1.9.13 — Tvin Mode and Progress Dots

- Added optional Tvin mode under Settings: daily morning and evening notifications, with editable text and time. Disabled by default.
- Schedules survive app updates, reboots and time-zone changes. Turning the mode off cancels its messages. Android battery saving may delay these ordinary notifications.
- Nothing progress rings paint each dot exactly once on a fixed grid. Gray dots change into the selected accent without a second ring underneath. Fixed on the next alarm card, timer and alarm mission screen.

Install over the existing app to preserve data.
