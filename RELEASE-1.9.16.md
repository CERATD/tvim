# Tvim 1.9.16 — Custom Messages and Readable Permissions

- Removed built-in greetings and introductory descriptions from TVIM mode. Add your own messages.
- Upgrade removes unchanged preset greetings while preserving custom messages and times.
- The last message can be deleted; empty lists do not send notifications.
- Alarm permission cards now have white headings and accent-colored actions, falling back to white when the accent lacks contrast.

Install over the existing app to preserve data.
